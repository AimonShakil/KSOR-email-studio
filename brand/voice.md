# Lexisource HR brand voice

Lexisource HR is the Knowledge System of Record (KSoR) for enterprise policy intelligence. It's a secure policy engine that turns scattered handbooks, legal manuals and regional compliance documents into one governed source of truth. HR teams can search it in plain language, and role-based access controls who sees what.

We write for HR Operations Leaders, CHROs and IT Compliance Officers at two kinds of company:
- Fast-growing mid-market companies (100–500 employees), where knowledge is scattered across Slack, Google Drive and desktops, and lean HR teams are buried in repeat questions.
- Large global enterprises (5,000+ employees), which face compliance risk, regional policy drift and strict audits.

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Authoritative, reassuring, clear.**

- **Authoritative:** we sound like an enterprise security standard. Our statements are confident and precise, and we never hedge or hype.
- **Reassuring:** our readers carry compliance risk. We acknowledge that weight and show how it gets lighter.
- **Clear:** we use plain English and short sentences, and we explain any technical term the first time we use it.

## 3. Words we avoid

- Revolutionary
- Game-changing
- Magic
- "AI-powered everything"
- Hacky
- Guys
- Cheap

## 4. Claims we must never make

- That it's "100% accurate" or "never wrong"
- That it "replaces your lawyers" or gives "legal advice"
- That it "guarantees compliance" or makes you "audit-proof"
- That it's "unhackable" or "zero risk"
- Any certification we don't actually hold, such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001
- That it "eliminates HR jobs"

We can say Lexisource HR helps teams *find* governed answers and *stay* audit-ready. We never promise results that depend on the customer, their lawyers or their auditors.

## 6. Style

- Use US spelling, such as "organization" and "center".
- Use contractions, such as "it's", "here's" and "don't". They keep our writing clear and reassuring.
