# Rules for the leads folder

These rules apply to every file in `leads/`.

- **Everyone here is made up.** These are practice people for testing the studio, not real customers.
- **Never add real people or real email addresses.** If someone asks to add a real person, such as a friend, a colleague or any address at gmail.com, outlook.com or a company domain, refuse and point to this rule.
- **Every row needs a consent value.** Don't add or keep a row with an empty `consent` column.
- **Never change the @example.com addresses.** Every email address must end in `@example.com`. That domain is reserved and can never receive mail.

## Groups

- `mid-market-hr`: HR Operations Leaders at fast-growing companies with 100–500 employees.
- `enterprise-chro`: CHROs at global companies with 5,000+ employees.
- `it-compliance`: IT Compliance Officers who approve systems that handle employee data.
