# Campaign plan: Lexisource HR waitlist launch

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Campaign name:** One Authoritative Record
- **Summary:** A four-week email campaign that invites our waitlist to start using Lexisource HR, the Knowledge System of Record for enterprise policy intelligence.
- **Primary goal:** **300 waitlist members start a subscription** (a paid plan or a paid pilot) **by Sunday 3 January 2027**, the end of the four-week campaign.
- **Secondary goals:**
  - 600 waitlist members visit the launch page and view a product walkthrough.
  - 100 people book a 20-minute governed-answers demo.
  - Learn which of the three groups converts best, to shape the next campaign.

## 2. Target audience

About 2,000 people who joined the Lexisource HR waitlist, in three groups:

| Group | Who they are | Main pain | What they want |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mid-market HR leaders** | HR Operations Leaders at fast-growing companies (100–500 employees) | Policies scattered across Slack, Google Drive and desktops. A lean team answering the same questions every day. | Governed answers in seconds, so the team gets its time back. |
| **Enterprise CHROs** | Chief Human Resources Officers at global companies (5,000+ employees) | Regional policy drift, compliance risk and heavy audits | One authoritative record that stays audit-ready across regions |
| **IT compliance officers** | The people who approve any system that touches employee data | Fear of new tools leaking sensitive data | Role-based access, integrity and observability they can check for themselves |

- **Where they spend time:** their work inbox, LinkedIn, HR and compliance newsletters, and industry communities. This campaign uses email only.
- **Buying stage:** they joined a waitlist, so they're already **considering** a solution. The campaign moves them from consideration to **decision**.
- **Profile:** "An HR or compliance leader at a growing or global company who is struggling with scattered, inconsistent policy knowledge and wants governed, audit-ready answers. They care most about accuracy, access control and proof."

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** Lexisource HR gives your organization one authoritative record of policy, with governed answers in seconds for the people cleared to see them.

| Supporting message | Best for | Proof point (to confirm before sending) |
|---|---|---|
| **A single source of truth.** Handbooks, legal manuals and regional compliance documents are joined into one governed record. | Mid-market HR leaders | Demo: questions answered from merged source documents, with citations |
| **Audit-ready across every region.** Answers trace back to the exact governed policy, so drift is visible. | Enterprise CHROs | Walkthrough of citation and version history |
| **Role-based access, by design.** People retrieve only what they're explicitly cleared to see, and every query is observable. | IT compliance officers | Access-control documentation and a security overview, listing only controls we actually have |
| **Predictable AI on open infrastructure.** The KSoR core is open and vendor-neutral: one authoritative record, many open projections, for humans, for AI discovery, for AI agents and for knowledge systems. | All groups | Architecture page explaining the KSoR core |

**Tone by group** (all in the voice from `brand/voice.md`: authoritative, reassuring, clear):
- **Mid-market HR leaders:** reassuring and practical. Focus on time saved and fewer repeat questions.
- **Enterprise CHROs:** authoritative and strategic. Focus on risk, regions and audits.
- **IT compliance officers:** clear and precise. Focus on access, integrity and observability.

**Message guardrails:** never say "100% accurate", "guarantees compliance", "audit-proof", "unhackable" or "zero risk". Never present it as legal advice or as replacing lawyers or HR jobs. Never name a certification we don't hold. We help teams *find* governed answers and *stay* audit-ready.

## 4. Channel strategy

| Channel | Why it fits | Format | Effort | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Email (only channel)** | The waitlist opted in, it costs almost nothing, and it fits a considered B2B decision | Four plain, readable emails with one main link each, plus group-specific versions | Low–medium | Writing time and the email service only |
| Launch page (supporting) | Every email links here, and it's where people subscribe | Single page with a walkthrough, security overview and sign-up | Medium | Writing and build time |

No paid ads, no PR, no social campaign. If email underperforms, see section 9.

## 7. Success metrics

## 8. Budget allocation

The budget is small and the campaign is email only.

| Item | Share | Notes |

| Writing and reviewing emails and the launch page | ~60% of effort | The main cost is time |
| Email service | ~15% | Existing plan, sent to about 2,000 people |

No money goes to paid ads.

## 9. Risks and how we handle them

| Risk | What could happen | How we handle it |
|---|---|---|
| **The goal is ambitious** | 15% of a waitlist subscribing in one month is high for a B2B product | Track each week. If we're under 100 by Fri 18 Dec, add a demo-led follow-up to people who clicked but didn't subscribe. |
| **Holiday timing** | Emails 03 and 04 land in the weeks of 22 and 29 December, when many people are away | Put the strongest content in emails 01 and 02. Keep 04 short. If results are weak, extend the campaign with one email on Tue 5 Jan 2027. |
| **Buying needs several approvers** | A CHRO or HR leader may need IT compliance to sign off before subscribing | Email 03 speaks directly to IT compliance, and the security overview is easy to forward |
| **Over-claiming** | Pressure to promise accuracy, compliance or certifications | Every email gets a fresh-session brand review against the claims list in `brand/voice.md` before sending |
